Implement a basic pistol combat system.

PLAYER INPUT
- Aim with the mouse.
- Fire with the left mouse button.
- Support holding the mouse button for repeated firing, with
  a configurable fire rate.
- Prevent firing when the weapon is empty or reloading.

PISTOL
- Give the pistol a configurable magazine capacity, fire rate,
  reload duration, and damage.
- Display the remaining ammunition.
- Add a reload action using the R key.
- Show a brief muzzle flash when firing.

PROJECTILES
- Projectiles travel toward the cursor.
- Projectiles must collide with walls and stop when blocked.
- Projectiles must be able to hit enemies once enemies are added.
- Remove projectiles when they hit something or leave the world.
- Prevent projectiles from accumulating indefinitely.

DESIGN
Keep weapon parameters configurable.
Do not hardcode them across unrelated modules.

For now, create simple test targets so I can verify the shooting.

Do not implement enemy AI or additional weapons yet.

Validate the implementation, run the available checks, and explain
how to test firing, reloading, and wall collisions.
