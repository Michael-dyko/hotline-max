Implement melee combat and weapon pickups.

MELEE
- Add a melee attack activated by the Space key.
- The attack should affect enemies within a short distance
  in the direction the player is facing.
- Add a short attack cooldown.
- Give the attack clear visual and audio feedback.
- Prevent the same attack from hitting the same enemy repeatedly.

WEAPON PICKUPS
- Allow the player to pick up weapons from the ground.
- Add at least one alternative weapon, such as a shotgun.
- Make weapon statistics configurable.
- Display the currently equipped weapon and its ammunition.
- Define what happens to the previous weapon when a new one is picked up.
- Add a simple way to drop or discard a weapon if appropriate.

Keep the implementation small and compatible with the existing
combat system.

Do not introduce an inventory framework.

Validate melee range, cooldowns, weapon switching, and ammunition.
