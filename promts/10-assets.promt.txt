Integrate the supplied visual assets into the existing game.

FIRST, INSPECT THE ASSETS
- List the supplied files.
- Identify image formats, sprite sheets, tilemaps, and animations.
- Inspect available image dimensions and sprite sheet layouts.
- Identify any missing or incompatible assets.
- Do not guess animation frame layouts without inspecting the files.

INTEGRATION
- Replace placeholder player graphics with the supplied player sprites.
- Replace enemy placeholders with suitable enemy sprites.
- Replace the level's placeholder geometry with the supplied tiles.
- Add appropriate animations where sprite sheets support them.
- Preserve all existing movement, collision, combat, and AI behavior.
- Keep visual dimensions consistent with the logical game scale.
- Ensure the player's aiming direction and weapon visuals remain clear.
- Preserve placeholder graphics as a fallback when practical.

ASSET ORGANIZATION
- Keep asset files under public/assets/.
- Use clear, consistent asset keys.
- Avoid duplicating assets.
- Keep asset-loading logic organized.
- Handle missing assets gracefully during development.

IMPORTANT
Do not modify or redraw the supplied assets.
Do not add unrelated assets or change the art style.
Do not rewrite working gameplay systems merely to integrate sprites.

After integration:
1. Run the build and type checks.
