Implement the first enemy AI system.

CONTEXT
The player can move, aim, and shoot. The level contains solid walls.
Preserve existing mechanics.

ENEMY TYPES
For now, implement one enemy type armed with a pistol.

AI STATES
Use a simple, explicit state machine:
- PATROL
- ALERT
- CHASE
- ATTACK
- DEAD

BEHAVIOR
- Enemies patrol between predefined points.
- Enemies detect the player within a configurable range.
- Solid walls block line of sight.
- Enemies can investigate a recent disturbance, such as a gunshot.
- If an enemy detects the player, it becomes alerted.
- If appropriate, it pursues the player or attacks from a suitable
  distance.
- Enemies must not shoot through solid walls.
- Enemies should have a short reaction delay before firing.
- Enemies should stop acting after death.

COMBAT
- Enemies can take damage from player projectiles.
- Display a clear hit reaction.
- Remove or disable dead enemies safely.
- Prevent a single projectile from damaging the same enemy repeatedly.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
- Keep AI decisions separate from visual effects.
- Avoid running expensive perception checks unnecessarily
  on every frame.
- Make enemy movement and combat parameters configurable.
- Ensure enemies do not become permanently stuck on walls.

Do not implement advanced pathfinding yet.
Use a simple solution appropriate for a small level.

Test the implementation and explain the known limitations.
