Extend the game to support multiple levels.

ARCHITECTURE
- Keep level data separate from the core gameplay logic.
- Use a consistent format to define rooms, walls, doors,
  obstacles, enemy spawn points, and player spawn points.
- Reuse existing gameplay systems.

LEVEL FLOW
- Add at least three short levels.
- Each level should have a distinct layout.
- Each level should contain a reasonable number of enemies.
- The player should spawn at a safe, accessible position.
- The exit or completion condition must be clear.
- Completing a level should advance to the next level.
- Completing the final level should trigger the ending.

DESIGN
- Make each level short enough to replay quickly.
- Introduce difficulty gradually.
- Avoid placing enemies where the player can be hit before
  having a reasonable opportunity to react.
- Ensure all required enemies and objectives can be reached.
- Avoid accidental softlocks.

TESTING
- Verify every level's geometry and collision boundaries.
- Verify that enemies spawn at valid locations.
- Verify that the player can reach every required objective.
- Verify that restarting a level resets all relevant state.
- Verify that completing the last level triggers the ending.

Do not add procedural generation or an advanced level editor.
Keep the implementation appropriate for a small birthday game.
